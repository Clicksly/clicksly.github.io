<p>
  In 2023, my boyfriend cheated on me. Desperate to avoid solitude,
    I drove from Cincinnati to Chicago to stay with a friend.
    The next morning, my friend found a vintage digital organ on Craigslist in Gary, Indiana and he needed a ride.
    I took my camera and brought these photos back with me.
</p>
<p>
    Shot on Cannon M50. Early soviet era lens; 50mm f/1.8; unknown manufacturer.
  </p>
